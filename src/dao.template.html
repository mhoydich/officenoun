the office dao.

a place to draft a dao in office terms: a name, a purpose, some tokens, a treasury, a few rules. you get a charter out, as a memo and as json.

v0 only writes the charter. it deploys nothing and holds nothing.

what happens after the charter

v0 stops at the charter. to deploy one today, take the numbers to Homebase by hand: create the dao there, set the token, supply, voting period, quorum and deposit, and fund the treasury yourself.

a one-click deploy waits on a testnet run and a review. until both are done, nothing here will originate a contract.
