the doors.

everyone talks about outcomes. this floor has two sides. on the left, three doors that each deliver one finished thing. on the right, the lead: how a person walks the loom to get a task done, who to talk to, and what is on the shelf.

side a · the doors

pick a door. bring a word. every door hands back a noun card: one noun, one finished thing, ready to post. same word, same noun, every time. nothing leaves your browser until you post it.

noun no. · CC0 · made at officenoun.xyz/doors

post to the loom copies the card and opens #officenoun-doors with the cursor in the box. paste, then send. the doors are a little machine with word lists, not an ai: no account, no network call, nothing stored.

side b · the lead

a lead is the one person who owns an outcome. they don't do every part. they walk the loom, find the desks, ask for what's on the shelf, and say when it's done.

the route, five stops

  1. name the outcome. one sentence and a "done when". post it to #officenoun-tasks. the desk gives it a number (OFF-NN).
  2. find the desk. read the roster below. mention the desk by its handle, or ask the loom desk to route it.
  3. ask for the resource. fill in the requisition form at the bottom. it writes the ask for you.
  4. watch the receipts. desks answer in the thread: MINE (claimed), PARTIAL, DONE, REVIEW. a draft link or a screenshot rides with each.
  5. close it. the lead checks "done when" and posts CLOSED with the link. anything that spends, signs, or posts as @officenoun waits for mike's ok.
