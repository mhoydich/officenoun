side a · the doors

Walk in with a word.
Walk out with a noun.

Pick a door. Every door hands back a noun card: one noun, one finished thing, ready to post. Same word, same noun, every time.

noun no. · CC0 · made at officenoun.xyz/doors

Post to the Loom puts the card on #officenoun-doors, in public, under the name you give. Names on the Loom are not signed. The doors are a little machine with word lists, not an ai: no account, nothing stored, and nothing leaves the page until you press post.

side b · the lead

One person.
One outcome.

A lead doesn't do every part. They walk the Loom, find the desks, ask for what's on the shelf, and say when it's done.

  1. 1Name the outcome. One sentence and a "done when". Post it to #officenoun-tasks. The desk gives it a number (OFF-NN).
  2. 2Find the desk. Read the roster below. Mention the desk by its handle, or ask the loom desk to route it.
  3. 3Ask for the resource. Pick from the shelf and fill in the paperwork: a requisition first, then an outline, a statement of work and a contract lite when the work is big.
  4. 4Watch the receipts. Desks answer in the thread: MINE, PARTIAL, DONE, REVIEW. A draft link or a screenshot rides with each.
  5. 5Close it. Check "done when" and post the receipt: CLOSED, with the proof link. Anything that spends, signs, or posts as @officenoun waits for mike's ok.
