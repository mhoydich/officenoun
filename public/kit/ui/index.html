office ui kit · v1 · cc0

One link tag.
The whole office look.

The look behind doors, faucets and pools, as one stylesheet anyone can use: big two-tone headlines, soft tiles, pill buttons and tabs, grouped lists and the hearth gate card. Light and dark follow the reader.

Start in a minuteSee every part ›

start

Paste two lines.
Add one class.

Everything is scoped under .ou, so it never restyles the rest of your page. The script is optional: it only adds the tabs, the two-tap buttons and the hearth.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://officenoun.xyz/kit/office-ui.css">
<script src="https://officenoun.xyz/kit/office-ui.js" defer></script>

<body class="ou">  <!-- or class="ou" on any wrapper -->

Theme it by overriding the custom properties on .ou: --ou-blue, --ou-warm, --ou-radius, --ou-font and the rest. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark". No build step, no packages, nothing phones home.
